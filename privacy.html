Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

PostFlow is a personal-use automation tool built and run by a single individual (the owner) to manage their own video posting. It is not distributed to or used by the general public.

01 Data it accesses

  • Google Drive — reads file metadata and content from a folder the owner designates, to detect new video files.
  • YouTube — uploads videos to the owner's own channel.
  • Facebook & Instagram (Meta) — publishes posts and Reels to the owner's own Page and linked Instagram professional account.
  • TikTok — uploads or drafts videos to the owner's own account via the Content Posting API.

02 How data is used

Data retrieved from these services is used only to detect new video files and publish them to the owner's own accounts. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or handed to any third party outside the platforms listed above.

03 Storage

Access tokens needed to connect to these services are stored locally in the app's own configuration and are not transmitted anywhere beyond the platforms listed above.

04 Revoking access

The owner can revoke PostFlow's access at any time through each platform's own settings — Google Account permissions, Facebook Business Integrations, or TikTok connected apps. Revoking access stops PostFlow from reaching that platform's data immediately.

05 Google API Services User Data Policy

PostFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
