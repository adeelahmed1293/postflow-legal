Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

PostFlow (“we”, “our”, or “the Application”) is an automated video scheduling and distribution tool designed to help creators publish video content from their designated cloud storage to connected social platforms. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, handling, and security practices.

01 Google User Data Accessed & Requested

When you connect your Google Account to PostFlow, we request permission via official Google OAuth 2.0 scopes (specifically https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). Specifically, PostFlow accesses:

  • File & Folder Metadata: File IDs, names, file sizes, creation/modified timestamps, and MIME types located strictly within your designated Google Drive folder to detect scheduled publishing dates and times encoded in video filenames.
  • Video Media Content: Video binary content streamed temporarily through memory directly to your authorized publishing destinations (YouTube, Facebook Pages, Instagram, TikTok).
  • Google Drive Trash Management: Permission to move processed video files to your Google Drive Trash folder (recoverable by you for approximately 30 days) in accordance with your configured retention cleanup policy.

02 Ephemeral Streaming & Zero Permanent Video Storage

PostFlow operates under an Ephemeral Streaming (Zero Permanent Storage) architectural model. Raw video files stored in your Google Drive are never permanently copied, retained, or stored on PostFlow’s servers or external databases. Video chunks are streamed directly in-memory from Google Drive to your designated social media endpoints and are immediately discarded upon transfer completion.

03 How Data Is Used

Data retrieved from connected services is used strictly to detect your video files, read filename timestamps, and publish videos to your authorized accounts according to your schedule. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data, nor do we share your information with advertisers, third-party data brokers, or marketing entities.

04 OAuth Token Security & Encryption

All OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens (for Google Drive, Meta, and TikTok) are encrypted at rest using industry-standard symmetric cryptography (Fernet AES-128 / HMAC-SHA256) before storage in our database. Tokens are transmitted strictly over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections by automated background worker tasks solely to execute your scheduled publishing requests.

05 Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

PostFlow’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements, including personalized, retargeted, or interest-based ads.
  • We do not sell, transfer, or disclose Google user data to third parties outside of executing your direct publishing commands.
  • We do not use Google user data to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Human personnel cannot read or view your Google Drive video content unless explicitly requested by you for technical troubleshooting or required by law.

06 Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting from PostFlow: You can disconnect your Google Drive connection at any time via the PostFlow dashboard. Disconnecting immediately and permanently deletes all stored access tokens, refresh tokens, and folder IDs from our database.
  • Revoking Access via Google: You can revoke PostFlow’s permissions at any time directly through your Google Account Security Permissions page.
  • Google Drive Files: Your original files remain in your personal Google Drive account. Any files moved to Google Drive Trash remain recoverable by you for approximately 30 days and can be restored at any time.
