Moves a video from a Drive folder to the platforms it belongs on.

PostFlow is a small personal automation tool. It watches one Drive folder and publishes new videos to the owner's own accounts — nothing more.

01 What it does

A new video file appears in a designated Google Drive folder. PostFlow detects it and publishes it to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts — run by and for one person, on their own content.

02 Who it's for

Just the owner. PostFlow is not distributed, sold, or offered as a service to other users.

03 What it touches

  • Google Drive — reads video files from one folder.
  • YouTube — uploads to the owner's channel.
  • Facebook / Instagram — publishes to the owner's Page and linked account.
  • TikTok — uploads or drafts to the owner's account.
